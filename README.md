## Splashlog

## Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: June 2026**

## Overview
SplashLog ("we", "our", or "the app") is a pool maintenance and chemical tracking application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is stored, and your rights.

## Data We Collect
SplashLog collects only the data you enter into the app, including:

* Pool names and settings
* Water chemistry test readings
* Chemical addition logs
* App preferences (theme, temperature unit)

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

## How Data Is Stored
To provide a seamless experience across devices, your pool data is securely synchronized and stored in Supabase database. This infrastructure ensures your pool records are backed up and accessible whenever you sign in. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Analytics & Error Tracking
We collect anonymous technical data, such as crash reports and app performance metrics. This helps us improve the app. This data is encrypted in transit and does not contain any personal information that could identify you.

## Children's Privacy
SplashLog is not directed at children under the age of 13.

## Your Rights
You have full control over your data. You can view, modify, or delete your pool records at any time within the app. If you choose to delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from database.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the app store listing.
